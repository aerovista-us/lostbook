🌧 Weather

Panhandle seasons

weather · lake basin

The sky here works in systems, not moods.

Pacific moisture hits the Rockies first — north Idaho gets more rain and snow than much of eastern Washington in the same storm track. Then the lake smooths the edges in town. The mountains do not.

  • Fall: first serious wind + rain mixes; leaves clog drains; power blinks before snow commits.
  • Winter: wet snow, thaw, refreeze — black ice on bridges and shaded curves on US-95.
  • Spring: runoff loud in draws; trails soft; do not camp in low cuts “because it is sunny on the lake.”
  • Summer: dry heat, then smoke when forests inland burn. Orange sun means limit exertion and watch air quality.
Lake City note: July can hit the 90s°F in the valley while Lookout Pass still holds snow on the edges. Pack for two elevations if you cross I-90.

— R. Vale

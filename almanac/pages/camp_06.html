Morale Is Fuel (Not Fluff)

Groups don't die only from cold. They die from giving up while still warm enough to try. After water, shelter, and food are honest:

  • One small joy — tea, a song, cards, watching ravens argue.
  • No contests that humiliate the weakest — save strength.
  • Praise the boring jobs: dishes, firewood, listening watch.

Kids need a job and a story. Adults need sleep and dignity. Same campfire, different medicine.

— M.

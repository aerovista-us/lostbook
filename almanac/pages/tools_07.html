Sharp Knife, Empty Lap

A dull knife slips. A sharp knife demands attention. Cut away from arteries, never toward your thigh while sitting in a truck cab.

  • Strop or stone in the crate — five minutes saves stitches.
  • Close before you stand. Sheath before you walk.
  • If someone else is hurt, your knife is clean as you can make it — still not sterile.

Mara: "The blood you prevent is the meal you don't waste."

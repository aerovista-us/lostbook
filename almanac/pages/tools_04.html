Orange Bag In Every Pack

Orange bag in every pack

Thirty-gallon contractor bag weighs almost nothing until the night you need it — then it's the difference between wet sleep and a chance at dry.

One bag, four saves we've actually used:

  • Rain shell — head hole, arms out, don't seal your face.
  • Ground sheet — water stops wicking up through foam that felt fine at noon.
  • Gear dry-bag — maps, socks, the one phone you should have charged.
  • Signal panel — green boughs stuffed, hung where sky can see it.

Clear bags for solar stills and map sleeves. Duct tape around the cuff turns a torn hole into a seal good enough for one storm.

Lake City hardware: aisle end-cap, spring '32 — bought six, used five.

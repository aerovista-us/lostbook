Mistake #10

The Forecast Said Clear

Phone weather showed sun icons. The lake showed a line of cloud like a pulled zipper moving north. We left rain gear "to save weight" because the screen was confident and we wanted to be confident too.

Cold rain is worse than snow — someone wrote that in this book for a reason. We were wet, mean, and two days from dry socks. The birds knew before we did. (See Weather tab.)

Valley forecast is not mountain forecast. Lake forecast is not pass forecast. Check the sky like it can still lie — then pack like it can't.

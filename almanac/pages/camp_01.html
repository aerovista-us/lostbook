After the work is done

Games aren't childish. They're how a group remembers it's still a group when the work was fear-shaped all day.

Card rules written down so fights stay small. Whistles mean stop — even mid-laugh. No wagers you can't eat or fix. Winner washes the pot so pride stays cheap.

We kept a star-lane game half-finished in the back of this book. Ravens cheat. Kids finish the rules if adults forget how to laugh.

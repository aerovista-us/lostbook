💧 Water

The creek tells on the hill

Clear water at the bottom does not mean clean water at the top. Walk uphill before you drink. If the creek runs through old cut timber or a burned slope, taste nothing until you are above it.

The good bends are quiet — no foam, no smell of iron. I marked three on the inside cover map with a blue dot and a date.

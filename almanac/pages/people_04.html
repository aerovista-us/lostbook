Who To Trust (Slowly)

people · original hand

Trust is built in small deposits, not one dramatic vow.

Share a task before you share a bedroll. Share a meal before you share a cache. Share a name before you share a route that cannot be un-said.

Fast friends are fast for a reason. Slow trust survives the month when the grid fails.

Local note: Fernwood — never met anybody in a hurry here.

— R. Vale

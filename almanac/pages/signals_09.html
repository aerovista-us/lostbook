Whistle On Your Body, Not In Your Pack

Three blasts, pause, repeat — trouble. One blast — where are you. Two — come here.

If the whistle is in the bottom of your bag, it might as well be at home. Clip it where a glove won't bury it.

Mara: Most people waste words before they waste breath. Whistle first if you're lost in timber.

— R. Vale

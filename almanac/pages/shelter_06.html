Snow Loads A Tarp Like A Lie

Flat tarp in snow looks fine until it isn't. Mid-pitch sheds load. Shake lines before sleep and after every drift.

Tree well danger — don't camp under laden branches because the trunk looks strong. The snow around the trunk is a trap.

Mara: If the tarp sings, wind changed. Re-stake before you can't see your hands.

— R. Vale

I trusted the shortcut

I thought shorter meant safer. It did not. The cut looked dry on the map. On the ground it was boot-sucking mud and dead ends. Lost half a day and a heel wrap. Crossed out the line on the map with red — if you see this, take the long bend east.

Shortcuts are stories people tell to feel brave. The long path is usually long for a reason.

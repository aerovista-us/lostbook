Marks for Strangers

signals · copied pamphlet scrap

Copied from a trail board outside the old ranger station. Most of the board was sun-bleached. These were still readable.

Marks should be simple enough to understand tired, in rain, in smoke, or near dark. Do not invent clever marks when plain ones will do.

→

Go this way
Route continues.

×

Do not enter
Unsafe, blocked, watched, or contaminated.

○

Water nearby
Check before drinking.

△

Shelter nearby
May be temporary.

+

Medical help or supplies
May be empty by now.

!!!

Immediate danger
Leave or hide.

Pencil note: We added a small dot under marks we trusted. Two dots meant someone had checked it twice. Three dots meant winter changed it.

Good marks are placed where a person naturally looks: door frames, trail posts, bridge rails, flat stones, road signs, pump houses, and the inside of safe shelters.

Bad marks are too high, too hidden, too artistic, or too close to danger. A warning nobody sees is just decoration for the dead.

Do not mark food caches unless you mean to share them. A hungry stranger is still a stranger.

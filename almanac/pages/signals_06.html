Do Not Broadcast Everything

signals · correction

Earlier pages made us too generous with the air.

This is a correction. A hard one.

We used to tell anyone listening where the clean water was, where the bridge still held, where the children were sleeping, and which houses had medicine. We thought information wanted to be free.

Information wants to be useful. That is not the same thing.

Broadcast all safe routes so good people can find them.

Better rule: share safe routes with people you have reason to trust, or mark them quietly for those already close enough to need them.

  • Do not broadcast supply locations.
  • Do not name children, elders, or injured people over open air.
  • Do not describe defenses.
  • Do not say when a shelter is empty.
  • Do not repeat rumors as reports.
  • Do not answer a stranger just because they sound afraid.
Different hand: Fear can be real and still be bait.

The kind way is not always the loud way. Sometimes the kind thing is to carry the message by hand, leave a mark under a stone, or tell one trusted person instead of the whole dark.

New rule: Broadcast danger widely. Broadcast resources carefully. Broadcast people almost never.

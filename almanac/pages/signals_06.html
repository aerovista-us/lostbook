Mirror To The Sun

Mirror To The Sun, Not To Your Eyes

A signal mirror can reach aircraft miles away. Aim by putting the bright spot on your palm, then move to the target. Three flashes, pause, repeat — same rhythm as whistle (Three Means Trouble).

  • Works when voice doesn't. Fails in thick timber — climb to opening or don't waste energy yelling at trees.
  • Phone screen can backup — not as bright, still worth trying.

— R. Vale

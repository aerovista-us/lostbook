Keep one coal alive

Copied from a older page, edges burned: When fuel runs out, do not let the last flame die in the open. Nest one coal in dry moss, wrap it in bark, carry it like an egg. Walk slow. Breathe away from it.

A live coal is lighter than gathering kindling in the dark. I kept mine in the brass locket on a chain — ridiculous until the night froze and the locket was the only warm thing I owned.

Watch or run — the skin fork

Ugly is not the test. Pretty is not safe. The fork is whether the body is containing the problem or losing the fight.

Run lane (move toward help, not heroics): red spreading past a palm in hours · red lines tracing up a limb · fever with the wound · wound won't close and keeps weeping · animal bite on face or hand · "it doesn't hurt" on a deep burn

Watch lane (map it, log it, don't pretend):

  • Draw the edge in pen on skin or tape a photo outline on the bandage — compare at the same hour tomorrow.
  • Heat that stays local is different from heat that rides with fever.
  • Clean with safe water. Cover. Change dressing when it soaks, add on top — don't rip the clot off to "check."

Most skin trouble is watch lane until it isn't. The moment it isn't, you've already lost time being polite about it.

The First Lie Is Usually Small

people · original hand

The first lie is usually small enough to forgive.

Wrong time on the log. “Already checked” when they did not. “Plenty of water” when the jug was low. Small lies are how people test whether you are paying attention.

If you let the small ones slide, you are training them for the large one.

— R. Vale

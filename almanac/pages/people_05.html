The Helpful Stranger Problem

Earlier pages were too generous. Helpful is not the same as safe.

A stranger who appears exactly when you are tired, lost, or alone is sometimes luck. Sometimes design. Sometimes both and you still cannot tell.

  • Help at the road edge, not inside your shelter ring.
  • Do not reveal where others sleep on night one.
  • Trade in daylight. Leave gifts, not invitations.

— different hand, winter copy

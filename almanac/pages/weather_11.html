Wind Carries More Than Weather

weather · margin

Wind carries more than weather. It carries sound, smoke, smell of somebody else's fire, and the first hint that a line is down somewhere you cannot see.

When the trees start talking all at once, stake the tarp twice and check what is tied to the ridge line.

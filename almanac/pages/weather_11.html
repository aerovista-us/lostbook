Whiteout: Your Eyes Lie

Snow fog + flat light = cliffs you don't see, trees you walk into, partners you lose in ten feet. If visibility drops below "I trust my feet," stop.

  1. Link up — rope, belt, voice contact every minute.
  2. Shelter in place beats "just making the treeline."
  3. GPS and map are useless if you sprint without a bearing — write heading before you move.
Fourth of July Pass: whiteout, two hours, six feet apart, never lost each other — because we stopped.

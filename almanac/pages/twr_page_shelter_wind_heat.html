🏠 Shelter

Wind steals heat first

shelter · original hand

Before you build walls, break the wind. A low wall of stone or pack-sod on the weather side does more than a roof with gaps. Lie with your feet toward the source if you cannot fully block it — feet go numb before the chest does.

I learned this the night the tarp sang like a kettle and the fire leaned horizontal but still burned.

Do not sleep on the ground

Mama wrote bigger underneath: Ground pulls heat out of you while you dream. Even a hand-width of dry needles between you and the dirt changes the night. Stack what you have — pack, bark, extra shirt — before you stack blankets.

I slept on her coat once. Woke with frost on my side but not on my chest.

Tinder honesty

Dry grass lies. It looks ready and puts out smoke like a grudge. Shave fine. Bundle small. Protect from wind until the first honest flame catches.

Char cloth in a tin if you have one. Pencil shavings if you don't. Birch bark if you're lucky and not stealing from something that needs it.

Local note: Wallace gets winter before you think it should.

— R. Vale

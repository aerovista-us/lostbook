Smoke Season

weather · original hand

Smoke season is weather.

Orange sun. Ash on the windshield. Throat that will not clear. Treat it like a storm: limit exertion, filter air, know which direction is cleanish.

Do not pretend the lake air is fine because the water looks pretty.

Local note: Smoke came over the ridge from the south again.

— R. Vale

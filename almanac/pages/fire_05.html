Char Cloth In A Mint Tin

Punch one hole in the lid. Cotton cloth (old T-shirt) inside. On coals, not in flames, until smoke stops and cloth is black and soft. One spark lights it — saves matches when everything is damp.

Store with your tinder. Label the tin so nobody uses it for mints.

Eli said his grandfather did this on the St. Joe before "waterproof" matches existed.

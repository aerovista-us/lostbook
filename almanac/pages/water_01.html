💧 Water

Water First. Always.

Water First. Always.

People talk about food too early. Hunger makes you weak. Bad water makes you useless. No water makes you dead.

Moving water is better than still water, but better does not mean safe. Clear does not mean clean. Cold does not mean clean. Mountain water can still carry sickness.

Boil it when you can. Filter it when you can. If you cannot do either, think hard before trusting it.

Local note: Avery — water good. Bridge questionable.
Later note: If the water smells like fuel, chemicals, rot, or metal, do not try to be brave. Brave people die stupid around bad water.

— R. Vale

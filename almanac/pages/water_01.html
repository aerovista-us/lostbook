💧 Water

Water First. Always.

Water First. Always.

People talk about food too early. Hunger makes you weak. Bad water makes you useless. No water makes you dead — slower than the movies, faster than pride admits.

Moving water is better than still water, but better does not mean safe. Clear does not mean clean. Cold does not mean clean. Mountain water can still carry sickness upstream of wherever you knelt to drink.

Boil when you can. Filter when you can — know the limits. If you cannot do either, think hard before trusting it. Think harder before letting someone you love trust it because you were tired.

Local note: Avery — water good. Bridge questionable.
Later note: If the water smells like fuel, chemicals, rot, or metal, do not try to be brave. Brave people die stupid around bad water.

Still water first. — J.

— R. Vale

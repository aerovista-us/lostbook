The birds knew before we did.

Ravens went quiet an hour before the wind turned. Not superstition — pattern. When the forest birds stop first, then the squirrels, then even the jays — look at the sky and start closing tarps.

Smoke season: the hush is different. Less wind, more ash taste. You learn which silence means rain and which means fire.

Local note: Hayden — lake flat, ridge not.

— R. Vale

Mistake #3

The Creek Looked Clean

Clear. Cold. Fast over cobble. We were down to half a liter and the pamphlet said moving water is better — and tired brains hear better as fine.

By late afternoon three of us knew. Not dramatically — no collapse on the bank. Just the kind of cramping that makes you hate the person who suggested "just refill here." By dark we were counting who could stand watch and who needed the trees.

Moving water is not safe water. Beaver ponds sit upstream whether you see them or not. Camp waste finds creeks. A dead animal in a spring you didn't walk far enough to check looks like nothing from downstream.

Vale was right about the river. Wrong about the bridge at Avery. — M.
Local note: Avery — water good. Bridge questionable.

— M. · wrote this so nobody "refills quick" on my watch again

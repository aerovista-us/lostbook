The Lake Doesn't Care

place · Coeur d'Alene

The lake doesn't care.

It will look calm while the wind tears your tarp on the shore. It will look beautiful while the water still wants your heat.

The Lake City is not a person. It is a basin that changes the weather in your favor and against you without asking.

— R. Vale

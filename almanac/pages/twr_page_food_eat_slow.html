Eat slow when you find plenty

The worst mistake after a lucky find is eating like the luck will last. Your stomach forgets hunger faster than your body forgets the week before. Portion half. Salt the rest if you have it. Hide the rest where you will remember but others won't.

Plenty is a mood, not a forecast.

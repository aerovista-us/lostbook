Ice On Lines Means Ice On Roads

When power lines glaze and branches crack in still air, the county roads will follow within hours. Plan like the plow is already behind.

  • Chains in the vehicle before you need them, not at the pullout in sleet.
  • Assume bridges freeze first — see Bridge Marks.

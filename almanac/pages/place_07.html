Places people forgot

Still on maps. Still on mouths. Not always on signs. The Panhandle is full of names that outlived the thing they named.

  • Mill corners — Cataldo, Wallace hills — no mill, still called mill.
  • Campgrounds closed but walked — rings, permits, habit.
  • Pullouts with a permanent cooler ghost — somebody always stops here.
  • FS numbers — mean more than town names once you're past pavement.
  • Lookouts nobody claims — light some nights. Don't assume empty.

Abandoned is not empty. Forgotten is not safe. Ask who still uses the name before you camp on the story.

Fernwood: never met anybody in a hurry here.
Old lookout: light some nights. Nobody claims it.

— R. Vale

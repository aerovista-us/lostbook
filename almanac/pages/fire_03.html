Smoke Kills Before The Fire Looks Big

People sleep in tents that smell like a campfire and wake up stupid — headache, nausea, can't think straight. That's not "tired." That's air without enough oxygen and too much carbon monoxide.

  • Never bank a fire inside a closed shelter. Vent high and low if you must heat with a stove.
  • If your headache goes away when you step outside, the shelter is the problem.
  • Wet wood smoke is worse than dry wood smoke. Move the cook fire downwind of where you sleep.

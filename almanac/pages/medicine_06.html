The umbles — when cold owns the brain

Hypothermia doesn't always look like a movie. The person still argues. The person still insists they're fine. Watch for the umbles:

  • Mumble — slurred words, stories repeating.
  • Fumble — can't zip a coat, drops the spoon, can't find the pocket they just used.
  • Grumble — irrational anger at the person trying to help.
  • Stumble — walks drunk without drinking.

Fix: get horizontal, dry layers, gentle heat (not fire on numb skin), sweet warm drink if awake and swallowing. No rubbing frostbitten skin. No heroic march to prove toughness — that's how stumble becomes don't-wake-up.

Cold rain is worse than snow in this book for a reason. Wet cotton in wind is a refrigerator you wear.

Pairs with: Dry layer page · Cold rain · Mistake #10 (forecast said clear).

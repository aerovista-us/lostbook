Moose own the road.

You are a guest on their commute. Dawn and dusk on US-95, on FS spurs, on the road to Priest Lake — assume antlers at headlight height.

Do not honk to win. Back off. Wait. A moose is slower than your impatience and heavier than your truck.

Is it a cow or a bull? — small writing in margin

Cow if you're asking. Bull if you're arguing. — M.

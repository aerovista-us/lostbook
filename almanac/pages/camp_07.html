Rainy Day Ledger

When stuck under tarp for hours, play ledger not argument:

  1. Inventory without blame — what we have, what we need.
  2. One improvement before dark — patch, dry socks, fix stove.
  3. One story that isn't about fear — Wallace sunrise, huckleberries, dog that knew first.

The book gets passed on when the rainy days didn't break people.

🔥 Fire

Smoke is a signature

A tall white plume is a letter anyone can read. Dry pine throws a column. Green wood sulks and smolders low. If you need warmth, build small under cover and feed it slow. If you need to be found, that is a different fire and a different day.

Never burn where the wind carries straight to the road unless you mean to be seen.

Margin: trader: "saw your smoke — thought rescue, found soup" · later hand: circled "different day" twice
Relic: charred forked stick (fire poker) · Where: leeward shelf above the gravel wash · Use: Match fire size and fuel to visibility vs concealment needs · Meaning: Fire is never only heat — it is also who you are willing to summon

— R. Vale

Snow Is Not Free Water

Eating snow cools your core. Melting snow in your mouth steals heat you needed for thinking. Melting a pot of snow takes fuel and time you didn't budget.

  1. Start with a little water in the pot, then add snow — melts faster, scorches less.
  2. Collect clean snow from the top layer, not yellow, not trail-packed.
  3. Still boil or treat if you can — animals and exhaust exist even where it looks pristine.
See also: Water First. Always. · Cold Shock (lake, not just snow).

— R. Vale, winter margin

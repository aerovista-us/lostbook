Lake & river — cold water

water · lake country

Notes from boat ramp talk & old Kootenai County health insert — not the glossy tourism version.

Lake Coeur d'Alene and the Spokane River are why people stay. They are also how people get hurt when they treat big water like a warm pond.

  • Surface can be warm in July. Depth is still cold. Minutes matter if you go under.
  • After storms, assume runoff and debris near mouths and marinas.
  • Blue-green algae blooms happen — if the water looks wrong and smells wrong, do not use it for anything.
  • Shore ice in late fall / early spring lies about thickness. Stay off unless you know the spot and the week.
Boil or filter — same as everywhere else. Pretty water is not clean water.
Fuel note: Marina spills and old fuel near docks — if it smells like gas, do not boil it clean. Walk away.

— M.

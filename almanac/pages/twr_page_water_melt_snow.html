Melt snow before you trust the cup

Snow looks clean. It is not a filter. Melt first in the tin, then raise to a rolling boil if you have fuel. If fuel is low, melt twice and drink the second run only. The first cup takes the road dust.

Marked the good tins with a single scratch on the handle — left side means "boil twice."

Margin: later hand: "still true in the pass" · trader: swapped tin 14 for cloth — fair

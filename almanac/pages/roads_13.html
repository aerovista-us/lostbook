Mistake #12

We Trusted The GPS

The blue line crossed a washout that existed on the ground and not on the screen. The screen was certain. The axle was not.

We had a torn FS map fragment in the glove box. We had eyes. We had a quarter mile of doubt we didn't walk because the arrow said go.

Paper + eyes + walking the doubtful section beats certainty from a dying battery. When the phone dies, the plan starts — not the panic scroll.

Do not eat this

Hunger makes every leaf look like food. This list is for when the pantry is gone and pride is loud.

  • White berries, red stems — no debate in the Panhandle. (See huckleberry note on Pantry Before Plants.)
  • Anything from stagnant, foam-edged water — even "cooked quick."
  • Mushrooms you wouldn't bet a friend's life on — false morels don't care how confident you sound.
  • Meat that smells sweet instead of merely bad — sweet is rot talking.
  • "It was cold so it's fine" — cold slows bacteria, doesn't pardon them.
  • Plants with milky sap you didn't 100% ID — wait one meal. Hunger is loud. Poison is quiet.
Vale margin: learned the hard way near Priest River — not on this page. See Mistake #2 if the gut goes wrong.

— R. Vale

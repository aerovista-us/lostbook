Infection moves faster than pride

A small cut in creek water can own your week. The skin fork page is about watch vs run — this page is what run looks like when you waited too long on watch.

  • Red line tracing up arm or leg — move. Not after breakfast tomorrow.
  • Heat, pus, smell, fever together — not a debate topic around the fire.
  • Clean with safe water, cover, change when soaked — add pads on top, don't rip the clot off to peek.

Duct tape over dirt is not a dressing. It's a story you tell yourself so you can keep walking.

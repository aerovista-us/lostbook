Three knocks means come in

Copied from a doorframe at the old relay cabin: One knock — who is there. Two knocks — friend, wait. Three knocks — come in, fire is on. More than three — trouble, bring help or stay quiet.

We used it on tarp poles when the fog came in thick. Sound carries when sight fails. Teach it before you need it.

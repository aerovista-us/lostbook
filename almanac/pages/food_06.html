One pot, less risk

One Pot, Less Cleanup, Less Risk

Every extra pan is another thing to wash in scarce water. One pot meals: grain + broth + fat + whatever protein is honest.

  1. Toast grain dry first — tastes better, uses less fuel.
  2. Hot water second — not cold into hot oil (splatter, burns).
  3. Season at the end — salt hides mistakes you needed to see.

Less dishes = more sleep. — J.

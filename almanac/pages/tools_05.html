Duct Tape Is A Tool Category

Wrap a few feet around a water bottle so it's always on you. Uses that paid for themselves:

  • Blister hot spots before they open.
  • Pin a torn tarp until you can re-stitch.
  • Splint a finger to a stiff stick (not too tight — pink nail check).
  • Seal a cracked water bottle neck, temporarily.
  • Label jars so "salt" isn't "sugar" in bad light.

It fails in deep cold and on wet skin — carry cordage too.

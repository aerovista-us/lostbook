water · original hand

Mountain water can still make you sick.

Altitude does not bless water. Beaver ponds upstream. Camp waste. Dead animal in a spring you did not walk far enough to see. Clear and cold only describes how it looks.

Boil when you can. Filter when you can. When you cannot, remember the creek that looked clean.

— R. Vale

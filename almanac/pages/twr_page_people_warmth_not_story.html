👥 People

Share warmth, not your whole story

A stranger at the fire is not automatically a friend. Offer tea. Offer dry socks if you can spare them. Do not offer your route, your cache, or the names of people waiting for you.

Kindness costs less than suspicion and buys more than boasting. I have slept beside people I never named and woke safer for it.

Margin: R. Vale: "socks yes, maps no" · later hand: "still the rule at the crossing"
Relic: pair of spare wool socks, rolled · Where: shared fire ring, mile before the bridge · Use: Bounded hospitality — help without exposing vulnerabilities · Meaning: You can be good to someone without handing them your life

— trader

Give soup before names. Give fire before history.

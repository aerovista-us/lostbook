Vent The Shelter Or Breathe Your Own Mistake

Sealed tight feels safe. Sealed tight + stove + people = condensation, CO, and that headache that "clears outside."

  • Low vent and high vent — air moves, frost doesn't eat the tent.
  • Never cook inside a closed space you sleep in.
  • Snoring is good. Silence when everyone should be asleep sometimes means trouble — check breathing.

Storm before dark

Storm before dark beats hero after dark. If clouds stack like shelves and wind goes flat, stake everything twice and move loose gear inside the ring.

In the basin, wind often hits before the rain finishes — check the Place tab scrap on east-lake outages before you trust one forecast for the whole county.

First rain is dust. Second rain is commitment. If you smell ozone and metal, get off high ground and stop arguing about tents.

Sprains: RICE, not heroics

"Walk it off" turns a week of limping into a month of useless on forest roads. Ankles are currency out here — spend them on the ridge you needed, not on pride.

  1. Rest — weight off. Craft a crutch before sunset, not after you're three miles in.
  2. Ice — snow in a bag, 20 on / 20 off if skin is intact. River cold counts if you can stand it.
  3. Compression — snug wrap, toes pink, pulse you can find.
  4. Elevation — above heart when camped.

Not this page: bone grinds, foot points wrong, numbness that won't leave, pain when you press the bone not the ankle. That's run lane — see skin fork.

Honest machine, honest ankle. Don't fake either. — Eli

— R. Vale

📍 Place

Lake, river, roads

place · trade + road scrap

Mile markers and pass reports traded at a gas stop on US-95 — better than trusting memory in shoulder season.

Water, ice, and asphalt share the same county but not the same rules.

Lake & river

  • Lake Coeur d'Alene looks calm from the road. It is still cold — hypothermia in summer is a local embarrassment people die of.
  • After heavy rain or spring runoff, creek mouths and the Spokane River rise fast. Clear yesterday is not clear today.
  • Do not drink lake or river water without treating — giardia and fuel spills both happen near marinas and old sites.
  • Ice near shore can be thin when the main lake still looks open. Test near land you trust.

Roads & passes

  • I-90 east to Lookout Pass — check Idaho / Montana 511 before you leave. Snow closes miles with little warning.
  • US-95 — long north–south spine; black ice when rain hits cold pavement in fall.
  • Forest roads into Coeur d'Alene National Forest — soft when wet, dusty when dry, wrong when you have no spare and no turnaround plan.
  • Rail beds and old logging roads (see Roads tab) — useful until they are not.
Field rule: Spokane is 30 miles west on paper. Weather and power can be a different country the same afternoon.
Smell note: Smoke fills the basin some summers — treat heavy haze like storm weather: stay inside, filter air, do not exert in orange sky.

— R. Vale · amended near Sherman Ave

The honest hydration check

Your body lies last. By the time you're cranky and making bad route calls, you were already behind at breakfast.

Urine (embarrassing, useful):

  • Pale straw — probably fine.
  • Apple juice — drink now, not after the next ridge.
  • Dark tea — you are working on yesterday's deficit.
  • Nothing half a day — medical territory, not toughness.

Headache + irritable + stopped sweating in heat — stop in shade, drink, salt a little, then decide anything important.

Coffee and beer are choices, not water. Someone crossed that out and wrote fight me. Drink water anyway.

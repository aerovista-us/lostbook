Pantry Before Plants

Hunger makes every leaf look edible. Exhaustion makes you skip the ID steps. If you still have rice, oats, or peanut butter, eat that first — foraging is a skill with a casualty rate, not a personality trait.

Three rules before any wild bite:

  1. 100% ID from someone who has eaten it in this region — not a phone photo.
  2. Small test bite, wait hours — not a bowlful because you're embarrassed to waste time.
  3. When in doubt, see Do Not Eat This page. No shame in hungry caution.
Priest River: huckleberries yes — white berries on red stems, absolutely not.

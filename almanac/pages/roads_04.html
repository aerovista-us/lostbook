Forest Service Roads Have Two Numbers

roads · copied scrap

Forest Service roads have two numbers. One on the map. One on the gate. Sometimes they agree.

Washout near 437 — check again after thaw. Tower road still passable last April. Trail marker gone past the second switchback.

Write both numbers in your log. Future you will not remember which one the stranger meant.

Local note: Cataldo — old receipt used as bookmark.

— R. Vale

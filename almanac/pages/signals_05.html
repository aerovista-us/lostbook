When The Phone Dies

When The Phone Dies, The Plan Starts

Assume no signal at Priest Lake, up any FS road with three digits, or in a draw where the sky is a slit. Assume the battery dies because cold eats lithium and because you scrolled maps at lunch instead of charging while the truck still ran.

The phone was a convenience. The plan is the tool.

  1. Written plan with someone sane — route, vehicle, color, when worry starts vs when to send help. Same words on paper in the glove box. (People tab.)
  2. Whistle on body — three blasts, pause, repeat. Not in the bottom of the pack.
  3. Mirror or lid to sun — aim the bright spot on your palm, move to target.
  4. Stay put if they know your route — moving targets are harder than camps. Moving without a bearing is Mistake #4 territory.
  5. Radio windows — dawn, dusk, after storms. Height beats volume.

A signal is a door. Don't open every door to strangers. (Mistake #5.)

— R. Vale

Do Not Camp In The Draw

shelter · correction

Do not camp in the draw.

North Idaho draws look sheltered. They collect cold air, runoff, and smoke like a bowl. One hard rain and you wake in a creek you did not hear arrive.

Camp on the shoulder above the draw. Wind you can pitch against. Water you can walk down to on purpose.

Local note: Spirit Lake — ridge wind lies about the valley.

— R. Vale

Learned this the wet way. — M.

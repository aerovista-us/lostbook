The Dry Layer Beats The Warm Layer

The dry layer beats the warm layer

People stack blankets while they're still sweating into cotton. Wet cotton in wind is a refrigerator you wear. Fix wet first, then worry about thick.

  • Get off the ground — pad, boughs, foam, cardboard. Conduction steals more than you think.
  • Vapor barrier — trash bag, poncho, tarp between you and wind even inside a tent.
  • Loft only works when it's dry loft. Compress wet insulation and it's a damp towel with ambition.
Margin: If your socks are wet, your day is lying to you. — Mara

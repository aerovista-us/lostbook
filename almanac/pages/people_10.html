Tired People Lie (Including You)

Sleep debt makes strangers into threats and friends into enemies. Before any hard call about trust, food split, or route:

  • Did everyone eat? Drink? Sleep at least a little?
  • Postpone the vote till morning unless weather forces now.
  • One person on watch who isn't the angriest — rotate.

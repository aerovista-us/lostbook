Ridge line knot

Learned from Mara at the river camp — traded for peaches, kept the words:

Ridge line parallel to wind. Doors leeward.

Not poetry. Geometry. Wind hits the long side of a tarp like a sail. Run your ridge with the wind, not across it. Put the door on the downwind edge so smoke and spindrift don't walk in with you.

If the tarp sings like drums, one of two things happened: lines too tight (tarp can't flex), or wind shifted and you're now a flag. Re-stake before dark, not after you can't see your hands. Night re-stake is how people fall off slopes they were fine on at lunch.

Peach cans bought the trucker's hitch diagram on the Tools page. Worth it. This page is the part you read when it's already raining.

— Mara

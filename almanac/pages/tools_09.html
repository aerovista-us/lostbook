The forest gets quiet first.

Birds stop. Then squirrels. Then the wind sounds wrong. Not magic — presence. Elk, person, bear, or weather; quiet means look up from the task in your hands.

Ravens found it first. You are only the second witness if you are paying attention.

— R. Vale

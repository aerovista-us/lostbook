Night Light Tells Two Stories

Flashlight to flag down help: good. Flashlight wandering the ridge at 2 a.m.: looks like searchers, invites wrong visitors, drains batteries you need for maps.

  • Signal pattern: three short, hold, repeat.
  • Camp perimeter: low red if available — preserve night vision and don't advertise supplies.
  • If you hear engines, light once, then listen — not a disco.
Mistake #5: told too many where we were — light is another kind of telling.

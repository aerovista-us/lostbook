💧 Water

Boil times (copied)

Copied from Kootenai County emergency pamphlet (Panhandle insert), ~2031

Boil water before drinking.

  • Rolling boil — 1 minute (flatland)
  • 3 minutes above ~6,500 ft if you track altitude
  • Let cool covered; label date + who boiled
Margin note: Unless it smells like fuel. Boiling fuel-water just makes poison soup. Learned that the hard way.

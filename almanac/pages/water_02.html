Slow is not cowardice

Slow is not cowardice.

A road on a map is only a promise. A bridge after a storm is a question. A lake in November is a decision you make with your whole body, not your schedule.

Move at the speed that lets you still answer if someone asks your name. Move at the speed that lets you stop before the draw, the ice, the moose, or the washout chooses for you.

Fast groups don't impress the forest. They impress the people who find their gear scattered along the wrong ridge.

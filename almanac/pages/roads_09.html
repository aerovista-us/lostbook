If You're Lost, Stop Making It Worse

Panic walks in circles. Circles burn daylight, water, and the one person who still trusts your judgment.

  1. Stop. Sit. Drink if water is safe. Eat a little salt and fat.
  2. Mark where you are — stick, rock arrow, torn bandana on a branch.
  3. Backtrack to the last place you knew — trail junction, creek crossing, mile marker.
  4. Only then choose a direction. Walking faster without a plan is how the North Road mistake happened.

Bank Coals, Not Your Breath

Ash pile under coals holds heat till morning. Good. A closed tent holding the same heat holds the wrong gases. Good fire discipline:

  1. Let flames die to coals before you sleep — less spark, less smoke.
  2. Cover coals with ash only if wind won't scatter them into the forest (see Smoke Season).
  3. Hot rocks in a pit can warm a shelter — never hot rocks + plastic + closed door.
Local note: FS draw near Athol — one escaped coal cost a whole ridge.

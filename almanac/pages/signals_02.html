A signal is a door.

Not every knock should be answered. A whistle, a lantern, a mark on a post — each one invites a stranger into your timeline. Signal when you need witness. Stay quiet when you need time.

Three means trouble. One answered three can mean rescue or ambush. Listen longer than you signal.

— R. Vale

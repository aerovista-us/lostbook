Filters and tablets have limits

Charcoal in a sock is better than nothing. It is not magic. Most field filters stop cysts and bacteria you can name — not everything that ruins a week.

Read the cartridge box like it matters, because it does. Replace on schedule, not on hope. A clogged filter is a strainer for things that fit through cracks.

Boil when in doubt — rolling boil, one minute at elevation you can hold (see copied pamphlet). Chemical taste beats giardia. Fuel smell, solvent sheen, or chemical rot in the water — walk away. No filter fixes that. No bravery fixes that.

Mistake #3: creek looked clean — read it before you "just refill."

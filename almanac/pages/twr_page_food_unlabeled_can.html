The can without a label

Found it in the rubble of a roadside cache. No label. Dented but sealed. Shake test: no slosh. Smell at the seam: sweet-metal, not rot. Open downwind. Spoon, not gulp.

If it is bad, you will know in the first breath. If it is good, mark the spot on the map and do not gorge. Unlabeled luck belongs to the next hungry person too.

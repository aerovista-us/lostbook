The helpful stranger problem

Earlier pages were too generous. Helpful is not the same as safe. A stranger who appears exactly when you are tired, lost, or alone is sometimes luck. Sometimes design. Sometimes both — and you still can't tell before you pay.

Help at the road edge, not inside your shelter ring. Trade in daylight. Gifts on the ground, not invitations through the door. Do not reveal where others sleep on night one. Do not correct this page out loud to a new face.

If they knew your route before you told them, you already made Mistake #5.

— R. Vale

Winter copy — still true. — M.

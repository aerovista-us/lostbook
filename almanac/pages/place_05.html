Winter in the Panhandle

Not one winter. Valley winter, pass winter, lake-effect winter. Downtown Coeur d'Alene can be workable while Lookout Pass is a different country.

  • December–February: wet snow, ice under tires, short days.
  • February mud: every logging road lies about being passable.
  • March thaw: culverts remember every mistake at once.
Local note: Wallace — snow arrives before you're ready.

— R. Vale

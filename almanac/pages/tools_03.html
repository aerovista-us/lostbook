Knot diagram

This knot diagram cost two cans of peaches. Worth it.

Trucker's hitch for tarps. Taught by a woman who never gave her last name. Drawn here so I don't forget under rain.

  1. Fixed loop on the anchor (tree, stake, truck rack).
  2. Run the free end to the tarp grommet, back through the loop, three wraps inside the loop (that's the pulley).
  3. Pull the working end — tarp tightens. Lock with two half-hitches on the standing line. Re-tighten after wind.

Not pretty. Pretty doesn't keep rain out.

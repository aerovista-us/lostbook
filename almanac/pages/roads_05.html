When Cars Stop Being Cars

roads · trade knowledge

Traded from Eli, who kept six dead trucks and one living bicycle behind the feed store. He said the bicycle was the most honest machine left.

A car is not useless when it stops moving. It only changes jobs.

Dead cars can still be shelter, storage, signal mirrors, metal, wire, glass, rubber, hose, cloth, bolts, containers, and warning barricades.

  • Mirrors: signaling, blind corners, watching behind you.
  • Seat fabric: padding, insulation, sling material.
  • Battery: power if charged, heavy hazard if leaking.
  • Glass: sharp tools, reflectors, cutting hazard.
  • Hose: siphon, water movement, repairs.
  • Tires: barriers, sandals, rubber patches, smoke signal if desperate.
  • Wiring: ties, snares where legal/ethical, repairs, binding bundles.
Later note: Do not sleep in a car in deep cold without cracking air safely and clearing the exhaust if engine runs. Quiet poison takes people in winter.

Running cars are loud promises. They tell everyone you have fuel, motion, maybe supplies, maybe fear. Sometimes that is fine. Sometimes it is a dinner bell.

Eli’s rule: Use wheels when distance matters. Use feet when being noticed costs more than time.

Bicycles became royalty after the pumps went quiet. No fuel. Less noise. Easy to hide. Easy to repair if somebody remembered how to patch a tube.

Mistake #8

We Skipped Sleep To Make Miles

Hour nineteen someone snapped over a spoon. Hour twenty-two we misread a junction and walked an extra three miles we didn't have in our legs or our water.

We weren't lost. We were stupid with fatigue — the kind that feels like urgency because stopping feels like failure.

Four hours horizontal. Split watch. Same map, different brains. The fix was never a better map.

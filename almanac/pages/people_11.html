Bears Are Not Villains

people · trade

Bears are not villains.

They are hungry accountants. Hang food. Clean dishes. Do not teach cubs that tents are pantries. Black bear in the Panhandle is common; stupid human is more common.

If you see one close: slow back, loud calm voice, do not run. Running is a receipt you cannot return.

Local note: Fernwood — never met anybody in a hurry here.

— Mara

travel · private mark code

Blaze trees and the forest remembers you forever. Scratch a stone and rain forgets by morning. I use three small notches on the back of my buckle — left fork means water, right fork means shelter, cross means turn back.

Anyone can read a blaze. Only the person who taught you the buckle code knows which way you went when the trail lies.

Mountain water can still make you sick.

Altitude does not bless water. It only makes you trust it faster because you're thirsty and the creek is beautiful.

Upstream of your cup: beaver ponds you didn't walk to, a camp's grey water, a dead animal in a spring seep, cattle on forest lease land, fuel smell from a road cut. Clear and cold describes optics, not biology.

Boil when you can. Filter when you can — know what the filter doesn't remove. When you cannot do either, remember Mistake #3: the creek looked clean.

St. Joe drainage: fast water, slow judgment.

— R. Vale

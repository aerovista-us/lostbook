Fever: When Staying Still Wins

Moving with a fever burns reserves and spreads whatever the group is sharing. Staying put with bad water burns everyone anyway — fix water first.

  • Child with fever + won't drink + won't wake normally → do not wait for morning out here.
  • Adult who was fine at lunch and can't stand at dusk → shelter, fluids, watch the dots (Third Dot page).
  • Fever breaking into sweat is not "cured" — it's a turn. Keep warm, keep fluids.

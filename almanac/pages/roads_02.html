Mistake #4

We Should Not Have Taken The North Road

It was faster on the torn map fragment. It was softer in reality — alder down across the tread, mud that swallowed the tread depth, and a creek where the bridge posts stopped but the water didn't.

We walked the last mile pushing. The axle didn't die heroically. It died on flat ground two days later because something in the undercarriage had been asked to pretend the north road was a road.

We told too many people which way we were going. That was mistake #5. This was the one that taught us: a road on a map is only a promise. (See Water tab — slow page.)

Eli margin (copied): Mark bad roads louder than good ones. — Eli

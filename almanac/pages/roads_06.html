Clear Highways Are Not Safe Highways

roads · correction

Earlier note said clear roads were good roads. That was lazy thinking.

A blocked road tells you what happened. A clear road only tells you someone had a reason to keep it clear.

Sometimes the reason is good. Trade route. Fire break. Evacuation path. Farm access. Sometimes the reason is a checkpoint, a toll, a trap, or a group that prefers visitors to arrive tired and visible.

Take the highway when it is open. It saves time.

Better rule: watch a clear road before using it. Roads remember movement.

  • Fresh tire tracks: someone still has wheels or carts.
  • Trash pushed aside: road is maintained.
  • Same footprints both directions: patrol or repeated route.
  • No birds, no small animals: people, smoke, dogs, or recent noise.
  • Fresh cut branches: passage cleared recently.
  • Road too clean near buildings: stop and study windows.
Different hand: Safe roads have boring edges. Dangerous roads feel watched.

If you must use a highway, do not walk the center like the old world still owns it. Stay near cover. Leave before dark unless you know where the dark belongs.

New rule: An open road is an invitation. Decide who sent it before accepting.

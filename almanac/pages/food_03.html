What To Eat When You're Scared

What to eat when you're scared

Your brain wants sugar and comfort. Your body wants calories that last and water that won't wreck you. Scared eating is how people skip step one and pay on step four.

  1. Water or safe hot drink first — dehydration feels like panic. Panic feels like a reason to skip water.
  2. Salt + fat + protein — nuts, peanut butter, dried meat, broth. Steadies you for hours, not minutes.
  3. Simple sugar last — quick lift, quick crash, bad decisions after. Save the candy for when you're already thinking straight.

Don't eat unknown plants because hunger is loud. Hunger is supposed to be loud. That doesn't make the mushroom friendly.

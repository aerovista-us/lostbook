Copied from three different bridge posts between mile marker 18 and the low farms. Same symbols appeared twice, so we kept them.

Bridges decide where people go. A town with one bridge is really a town with one throat. Check before crossing. Mark after checking.

B✓

Bridge held
Crossed on foot after last high water.

B?

Unknown
Visible, but not tested.

B×

Do not cross
Broken, trapped, watched, or unstable.

W↑

Water rising
Leave low road soon.

F

Foot only
Do not bike, drive, or pack heavy.

N

Noise risk
Loose boards, metal deck, dogs nearby, or watchers.

Look under the bridge if you can do it safely. Broken things hide underneath first. Check beams, supports, washed-out banks, fresh bends, hanging cables, and places where water has eaten the earth behind the concrete.

Pencil note: A bridge can hold one person and still betray six.
Best crossing time: daylight, low wind, low water, no hurry. Hurry makes every bridge worse.

If you cross, cross one at a time. Keep distance. Do not stop in the middle to talk about whether it feels safe. The middle is where the bridge gets to answer.

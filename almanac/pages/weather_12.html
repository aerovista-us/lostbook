The Sky Usually Warns You

weather · trade

Traded for a jar of huckleberry jam at a trailhead north of the lake.

The sky usually warns you.

Animals go quiet. Wind shifts. Clouds stack like shelves. People who ignore the first sign argue with the second.

Trust the sky before you trust the forecast for a town thirty miles away.

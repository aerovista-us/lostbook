Barometer apps lie when towers do. Old timers read joints, birds, and wind shift. Before the sky delivers:

  • Swallows low, woodpeckers quiet, lake chop against the wind you expect.
  • Hair stands on arms in summer — get off ridges.
  • Pressure drop + warm front smell = tie tarps, cache loose gear, fill water.

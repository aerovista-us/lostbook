Ridge line knot

Learned from Mara at the river camp:

Ridge line parallel to wind. Doors leeward. If the tarp sings like drums, lines are too tight or wind shifted — re-stake before dark, not after you can't see your hands.

This knot diagram cost two cans of peaches. Worth it.

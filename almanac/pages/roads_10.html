Night Driving After Trouble

Headlights turn moose into surprises and washouts into jumpscares. After storms, roads lie (see page 1). At night they lie louder.

  • Stop before you're weaving — cold cabin, bad decision.
  • Pull fully off — not a lane, not a blind curve. Mark the rear if fog.
  • Walk the questionable section in daylight if you must go on.
